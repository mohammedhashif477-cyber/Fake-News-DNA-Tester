Citizen-Clear Format

A widely circulated image and message claims that the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has mandated an emergency 5-day total shutdown of all private and nationalized bank ATMs starting tomorrow midnight, alleging acute digital currency liquidity shortages. The message urges users to immediately withdraw cash and forward the warning to at least ten contacts.

This statement is entirely untruthful. The attached gazette photo is an edited copy of an old public holiday notification from October 2021. The Reserve Bank of India officially issued statement PR-2025/112 confirming all clearing houses, digital payment gateways (UPI, NEFT, RTGS), and ATM cash-replenishment logistics are functioning at standard capacity without any unscheduled interruptions.

Primary Clarification Authority: Reserve Bank of India (Dept of Communication)
